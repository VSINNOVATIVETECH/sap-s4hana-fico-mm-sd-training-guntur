SAP S/4 HANA Courses

Pick one module, or combine them for a wider SAP skill set.

FICO

Finance & Controlling

For commerce graduates, accountants and finance professionals.

  • Enterprise structure and Universal Journal
  • GL, AP, AR and asset accounting
  • Bank accounting and period-end closing
  • Cost centres, profit centres, product costing
MM

Materials Management

For logistics, purchase, stores and supply chain candidates.

  • Material and vendor master data
  • Procurement cycle: PR, RFQ, PO, GR, IR
  • Inventory management and valuation
  • Pricing, release strategy, MRP basics
SD

Sales & Distribution

For sales, billing, dispatch and order management candidates.

  • Customer master and sales organisation
  • Order-to-cash: inquiry to billing
  • Pricing procedure and credit management
  • Delivery, shipping and returns
